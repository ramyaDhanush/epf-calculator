How it works

  • Interest accrues every month on that month's opening balance (before the month's own contribution), at rate ÷ 12.
  • Accrued interest is credited once, on 31 March, and earns interest from the following month. It is not compounded within the year.
  • Rows are financial years (April to March). The first and last rows are part years if you start or finish mid-year.
  • Enter only the employer's share that goes into EPF. The EPS pension share (8.33% of wages, capped at ₹15,000 wages) does not reach the EPF balance.
  • This is an estimate. Rates are set by EPFO each year and your actual passbook can differ by a few rupees.

Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
